Parking tipKings Park's own Wadjuk Carpark is free but capped at 4 hours on weekdays (7am–4pm) — not enough for a Kings Park + CBD day. Park in West Perth instead ($5.40/hr, cheaper than the CBD's $7/hr) and hop on the free Blue CAT bus, which runs between West Perth/Kings Park and Elizabeth Quay/the CBD all day — one parking spot covers the whole day.
